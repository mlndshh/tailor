Source: https://app.notion.com/p/coderabbit/JEVATHON-Jev-Hackathon-SF-3e796e76cda18143b74af9944bc5cddc
(fetched 2026-09-26). The Notion page is the source of truth; some sponsor prizes were still "to be confirmed".

## Key facts

- **When:** Saturday, September 26, 2026, 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM PT (rooftop afterparty until 8:00 PM)
- **Where:** CodeRabbit HQ, San Francisco (201 Spear, per the lounge wifi name)
- **Hosts:** TypeSafe AI (Jev) × The AI Collective, hosted at CodeRabbit
- **Hacking window:** 11:30 AM – **2:30 PM hard stop** (3-hour sprint)
- **Submit on HackerSquad before 2:30 PM.** Late submissions cannot be judged.
- **Main track requirement:** the project must be built with Jev. Partner tools qualify you for sponsor awards.
- Theme: "Build with Jev." New AI infrastructure, agentic workflows, or ambitious live prototypes. "The goal is not
  polished slides, but working code that could survive outside the room."

1. Register and sign up for the event:
   https://hackersquad.io/builders/dashboard/events/cmuhxoc83006kpl0k7au0d7ro/builder
2. Submit the project under the `./project.sh` panel
   (https://hackersquad.io/builders/dashboard/events/cmuhxoc83006kpl0k7au0d7ro/builder#project)
