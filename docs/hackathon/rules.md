# Rules, guidelines and team formation

Source: subpages of https://app.notion.com/p/coderabbit/JEVATHON-Jev-Hackathon-SF-3e796e76cda18143b74af9944bc5cddc
(fetched 2026-09-26, copied verbatim).

## Participation Rules

- **Team Size:** Teams should consist of 2 to 5 members. Individuals are welcome to participate but are encouraged to
  form teams for collaboration.
- **Main Track:** Projects must be built with Jev. Partner tools (CodeRabbit, Browserbase, Whop, Cognition,
  LlamaIndex, Photon, GMI, ElevenLabs) are encouraged and qualify you for the sponsor awards.
- **Allowed Tools:** Participants may use any open-source or commercially available tools, frameworks, and libraries,
  provided they have the necessary licenses and permissions. Pre-trained models are permitted, but your project must
  demonstrate original application or modification.
- **Original Work:** All submissions must be original work created during the hackathon. Pre-existing projects or
  unauthorized use of others' work is prohibited.
- **Submission:** Register your team and submit your project on HackerSquad before hacking ends at 2:30 PM. Late
  submissions cannot be judged.
- **Judging Criteria:** Projects are evaluated on the five dimensions in the Judge Grading Rubric
  ([judging-rubric.md](judging-rubric.md)):
  - Problem relevance and real-world value
  - Technical execution and engineering quality
  - Architecture and system design
  - Production readiness and feasibility
  - Clarity of vision and continuation potential

## Ethical AI Guidelines

As participants, you are expected to uphold high ethical standards in your projects. These guidelines ensure your AI
solutions are responsible and beneficial:

- **Fairness:** Ensure your AI models do not discriminate against any group based on race, gender, age, or other
  protected characteristics. Use diverse datasets and consider fairness metrics.
- **Transparency:** Make your AI systems explainable and understandable. Provide clear documentation on how your model
  works and makes decisions.
- **Accountability:** Take responsibility for your AI's outcomes. Consider potential misuse and implement safeguards to
  prevent harm.
- **Privacy:** Respect user privacy and handle data ethically. Ensure compliance with relevant data protection
  regulations.
- **Bias Mitigation:** Actively identify and address biases in your data and algorithms. Use techniques like
  fairness-aware machine learning where applicable.

For additional resources on ethical AI practices, explore IEEE's Ethically Aligned Design or ACM's Code of Ethics.

## Code of Conduct

We are committed to providing a safe and inclusive environment for all participants. Please adhere to the following
standards:

- **Respect:** Treat all participants, organizers, and staff with respect and courtesy.
- **Inclusivity:** Foster an environment where everyone feels welcome, regardless of background, identity, or
  experience level.
- **Zero Tolerance:** Discrimination, harassment, bullying, or any form of abusive behavior will not be tolerated.
- **Online Etiquette:** Maintain professionalism in all online interactions, including chat rooms, forums, and video
  calls.
- **Reporting:** If you experience or witness inappropriate behavior, report it to the organizers immediately. All
  reports will be handled confidentially.
- **Consequences:** Violations of the Code of Conduct may result in disqualification, removal from the event, or other
  appropriate actions.

This event is affiliated with The AI Collective. All attendees and organizers also agree to The AI Collective's privacy
policy and are subject to its code of conduct.

## Team formation

- Team formation runs **11:15 – 11:30 AM**, right after the opening session. Organizers run a quick round of intros and
  pitches.
- Teams should have 2 to 5 members. Solo participation is welcome but teaming up is strongly encouraged.
- Name tags show your name, profession or experience, and whether you are looking for a team, need team members, or are
  already set.
- Still searching after the formation round? Find an organizer and they will match you with a team.
- Post skills, interests and team status in the community Discord: https://discord.gg/NuJMa6VEa
